Frequently asked

Before you begin.

Can I create an account immediately?

Existing registered users can sign in. New customers can request access; account enrolment is currently controlled rather than open self-registration.

How does Sandy use my connected apps?

Sandy can access supported services only when the necessary account connection and permissions are available. Connecting one provider does not automatically grant access to every service it offers.

Does Sandy work through email and WhatsApp?

Email is a core Sandy channel. WhatsApp and voice are supported in certain configurations, with eligibility and setup dependent on the account.

Can Sandy act without my approval?

Some authorised tasks can be executed directly; sensitive or external actions may require approval. Sandy should distinguish a proposed action from a verified completed action.

Are the prices fixed?

Not yet. Sandy Personal, Pro and Business are enquiry-based offerings and no public subscription prices or checkout are currently offered.

What about autonomous improvements and advanced team features?

Those are roadmap capabilities, not guaranteed services. Current functionality is dependent on deployed integrations and account configuration.
