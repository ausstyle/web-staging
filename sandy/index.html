A few good questions

Before we get started.

Is Sandy just another AI chatbot?

No. Chat is one way to interact, but Sandy is designed around organising work across email, tasks, calendars, information and longer-running missions, with a focus on follow-through.

Can I start using Sandy today?

Existing customers can sign in. New customers can request access by email. Self-service account creation and automatic provisioning are not yet offered on this website.

Which apps can I connect?

Supported connections depend on your account and configuration. Permissions must be authorised for each relevant service. A connection does not imply unrestricted access to all data or services.

Can Sandy send emails or take action for me?

Where the relevant integration and permissions are enabled, Sandy can perform supported actions. Certain actions require your approval. You should receive a clear distinction between proposed, attempted and independently confirmed outcomes.

Does Sandy work completely autonomously?

Sandy's ability to plan, delegate and execute varies with the operation, permissions, safeguards and current rollout. Advanced autonomous features are being developed and should not be assumed available to every customer.

How much does Sandy cost?

Public subscription prices are not yet finalised. Contact us to discuss current access and commercial terms before committing.
