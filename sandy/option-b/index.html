Questions

Good to know.

Can Sandy connect to my email and calendar?

Supported account configurations can connect relevant services with your permission. We will confirm which connections are available to you before onboarding.

Does Sandy do the work or just recommend it?

Sandy is designed to coordinate and execute supported work where authorised, while distinguishing suggestions from verified outcomes. Some operations need your approval and advanced capabilities remain in rollout.

Commercial plans have not yet been publicly finalised. Ask for access and we will confirm pricing and terms.
