## Cash views, applied credits and canonical API integration

Implemented four cash-basis management views (TB, P&L, balance sheet, GL) as a deterministic projection of the accrual ledger. Each recorded payment stores exact original-line component allocations; allocation uses integer largest remainders against remaining budgets, and reversal removes the original snapshot. Credits against unpaid balances consume the same component budgets. A 110.00 document split into 100.00 net and 10.00 explicit synthetic tax recognizes successive 36.67 / 36.67 / 36.66 payments as net/tax 33.34/3.33, 33.33/3.34 and 33.33/3.33. No cash report posts a financial fact. Manual-journal cash inclusion is explicit and immutable on reversal. This does not implement statutory GST/BAS eligibility, rates or lodgment.

Applied customer and supplier credits now have scoped unique numbers, source evidence, expected versions, proportional line allocations, immutable journals, dated reversals and historical ageing. They cannot exceed the unpaid balance, overlap payment component budgets, predate settlement history or cross a period lock. Voids require settlement reversals first. Separate unapplied/contact credits, paid-balance refunds, reallocation and recurring lifecycles remain open.
